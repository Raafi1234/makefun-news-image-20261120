## Hero story

### 1. Russian missiles enter Poland during massive attack on Ukraine
Two Russian missiles and a jet-powered drone entered Polish airspace and came
down on Polish territory during a massive Russian missile-and-drone attack on
Ukraine overnight. Prime Minister Donald Tusk said the incursion did not look
like an accident but an "intentional provocation"; NATO called it a grave
violation of allied airspace. Russian strikes also hit three bridges in Kyiv,
killing three people and injuring about fifteen.

Sources: Reuters (via en.h.ua, 20 Nov 2026 08:39 UTC); The Independent live
blog, 20 November 2026; OneTurn daily brief, 20 November 2026.

## Other stories

### 2. Global markets plunge — S&P 500 enters correction
Wall Street fell sharply as the AI trade unravelled: the S&P 500 dropped 3.7%
to 6,910, its seventh straight loss, putting it more than 10% below its
record — correction territory. The Nasdaq fell 5.28% and Japan's Nikkei 5.06%.
Nvidia closed down about 14% at $125.53 after Reuters reported the US
Securities and Exchange Commission is examining its accounting practices.
Anthropic fell about 10% on its second day of trading after its record IPO.

Sources: Reuters, 20 November 2026; OneTurn daily brief, 20 November 2026;
Argus US brief, 21 November 2026 (recap of 20 Nov closes).

### 3. Houthi missile damages Riyadh's tallest tower
A Houthi missile struck the PIF Tower in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia's tallest
tower, blowing out windows across seven to eight floors. No injuries were
reported. The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted two Houthi drones
launched toward Riyadh and southern regions the same day.

Sources: YouTube daily-news summary "What Happened in the World in the Last
24 Hours | November 20, 2026"; Daily News roundup, 20 November 2026;
Tribune/Reuters, 20 November 2026.

### 4. US Senate blocks Iran war-powers resolution
The US Senate blocked the Iran war-powers resolution by 51 votes to 48.
President Trump said he was in no rush on the next step on Iran and would
decide "after January 1".

Sources: Daily News roundup, 20 November 2026; Reuters via Haaretz,
20 November 2026.

### 5. COP31 climate talks go into overtime
The COP31 climate talks went into overtime as nations quarrelled over fossil
fuels, with no agreement reached by the scheduled close.

Sources: Reuters, 20 November 2026 (via SCMP wire roundup).

### 6. Grand Theft Auto VI banks $3.79 billion in 24 hours
Rockstar Games said "Grand Theft Auto VI" recorded $3.79 billion in retail
sales in its first 24 hours on sale — a record launch.

Sources: Reuters, 20 November 2026 (via The Daily Guardian wire roundup).
